# Online services and the cloud — IGCSE ICT

Interactive lesson for Pearson Edexcel International GCSE ICT, **Unit 4 Online goods and services, Chapters 11 and 12** (book pages 168–179: online services, their impact, transactional data and cookies, hosted applications, online data storage, the impact of the cloud and the chapter questions). Only these pages are covered.

Each section is explained step by step with interactive simulations, followed by the book's **activity** (where the book has one) and a short auto-marked **✓ Check**. Each part ends with a **test** (multiple choice + exam-style questions with mark schemes, including all the chapter questions on pages 175 and 179).

**Part A · Online services** (Chapter 11, pages 168–175)

1. Shopping sites — product catalogue, categories and sub-menus, narrowing results, basket, checkout, voucher codes, account or guest, secure payment → Activity (Online shopping services) → ✓ Check
2. Booking systems — times, number and type of tickets, seat map (Figure 11.3) with another customer booking at the same time, basket for several trips and theatre tickets, email, e-ticket linked to a passport, a real scannable QR code → Activity (Booking flights online) → ✓ Check
3. Banks and education providers — balances, payments and transfers, balance alerts by text/email, loans, help, personal information; a VLE with library, online support and assessment → ✓ Check
4. Gaming, news and information — a multiplayer game hosted on a remote server, news subscribers and notifications on a smartwatch (Figure 11.4), real-time weather, sport, stock markets and currency → ✓ Check
5. Auction sites — live bidding, fixed price, ratings for buyers and sellers, non-payment and the second highest bidder, outbid and ending-soon alerts → ✓ Check
6. Impact on lifestyle and behaviour — time saved by online grocery, rail, banking, learning and information services; potential drawbacks; no connectivity; Table 11.1 → ✓ Check
7. Impact on organisations — communication, global markets, global workforce, big data, security, competition (world map) → ✓ Check
8. Transactional data and cookies — a browser with a cookie jar: what is stored, Set-Cookie and expiry (Figure 11.5), deleting, session, persistent and third-party cookies, an ad server and targeted banners, blocking third-party cookies, encryption → Activity (chapter question 11) → ✓ Check
9. Test Part A (chapter questions 1–11)

**Part B · The cloud** (Chapter 12, pages 176–179)

10. Hosted applications software — web app v local app with latency, bandwidth and no connection; fewer features; collaboration with chat and edits; Table 12.1 → Activity (Hosted and local software, Table 12.2) → ✓ Check
11. Online data storage — laptop, cloud, phone and a friend's tablet: every benefit and drawback of Table 12.3 → Activity (Choosing online data storage) → ✓ Check
12. The impact of the cloud — developers in a competitive marketplace, Chromebooks/netbooks and the digital divide, scalable services v own servers over a day, services and online communities for individuals → ✓ Check
13. Test Part B (chapter questions 1–4) · Key terms (searchable)

**Progress that students can share and resume** (same system as the other lessons)

- Saved automatically in the student's browser (`localStorage`, key `og-progress-v1`).
- **My progress** shows the percentage completed, with **Copy my progress** / **Download .txt** for the teacher, and a **progress code** (`OG1Z.…`) to carry on with another device.
- Nothing is sent to a server. Shops, banks, people and websites in the simulations are made up, and nothing can really be bought or paid for. Sound only plays after a click.

**Controls:** Play/Pause (Space), Next (→), Back (←), Restart scene (R), Projector mode (P), Full screen (F), Help (?). Each scene has its own URL, e.g. `#cookies` or `#check-storage`.

Visual style: British School Alzira · Xàtiva · Gandia (BS Format: navy, school blue, yellow step markers). Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step.
